A LETTER FROM MY HEART

To My Husband :

If you ask me why I chose you, and honestly, the answer is simple: because nothing in my life has ever felt more real than you.

At first, I fell in love with you so fast and so hard that I was almost scared, worried it was all too good to be true. But now? I know I want to fall in love with you even more.

Muel, you are my favorite person, and I still get butterflies when you take my hand. Being with you feels like coming home after a long, exhausting day.

You are where my heart finds peace, my safe place, and my answered prayer. I've never felt so loved, safe, and comfortable with anyone the way I do with you.

I love that you make me laugh, and that you know me so well. Loving you wasn't planned or logical, it just happened naturally, like breathing, and from that moment on, there was no turning back.

Every night, before I fall asleep, I pray the same prayer: Thank you Jesus, for showing me how deeply You love me by sending me a man who loves me so purely, and I believe You will always take care of Muel, the man who spends his life taking care of me.

Thank you for every sacrifice no one sees, every burden you carry in silence, and every time you choose our family over yourself.

I love the life and family we've built together. I want all the adventures, the big dreams, the ordinary days, and all the sunrises and sunsets in between. I want to keep growing with you, dreaming with you, and figuring out what this next chapter looks like together.

You brought smiles back into my life, and I'm so glad to be stuck with you. Even after all these years, I still want more life with you.

May God strengthen your heart, protect every step you take, and fill your life with peace and endless blessings. Amen 💖

Thank you for everything. I choose you. Still. Always. 💖
With all my love
